## What's Working

**The copy earns trust with measurements instead of adjectives.** The three `#` lines work because they reproduce the artifact of the thinking rather than describing it. The reader reconstructs the debugging themselves, which no amount of "sou detalhista" achieves.

**`.indent` is load-bearing design-system thinking.** Two properties applied to four unrelated blocks make "everything left-aligned" register as a decision rather than as absence of layout.

**The hairline construction holds under pressure.** `gap: 1px` over `--line` is used identically for the telemetry strip, the project grid and the contact row: three unrelated components, one technique, zero doubled borders at any seam. And the focus ring is a consistent 2px accent at 3px offset on every single interactive element, with zero exceptions, verified across 21 desktop tab stops and 18 mobile. That is better than most production sites.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The flagship dead-ends and contradicts itself
**What:** the ImóvelDF card is the largest block, carries the strongest evidence (364 tests, xdist in ~32s, CI/CD gate, `select_for_update` validated 10x against threads) and has zero outbound links. It closes on "Repositório privado enquanto o projeto não vai para produção." `github.com/Acciolyy/ImovelDF-Project` is public: `"private": false`, verified against the API, and reachable in two clicks from the GitHub link in your own contact section.
**Why it matters:** someone hiring for QA Automation goes straight to the test suite. The page says they cannot, then proves they can. Best case they miss the strongest evidence on the site; worst case the whole card reads as unverifiable.
**Fix:** link the repo from the card, ideally deep-linked to `tests/`. Or make it genuinely private and replace the note with something checkable: a CI run badge, the `pytest -n auto` output.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P0] Reduced motion is declared and not delivered
**What:** `observarVideos()` runs at site.js:371, before the `semMovimento` guard at 374. Measured under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: 3 of the 4 videos play and loop, none has `controls`. GSAP and the carousel are correctly suppressed; the videos are not. The carousel also does not pause on focus, arrow keys do nothing, and `#carro-legenda` is `aria-live="polite"` while auto-advancing, so it announces unprompted every 4.5 seconds.
**Why it matters:** WCAG 2.2.2, and you wrote this requirement into your own brief. A portfolio arguing "escrevo os testes que provam que elas continuam funcionando" that fails its own declared requirement is the most expensive bug it could ship, and it is ten seconds away in DevTools.
**Fix:** move `observarVideos()` inside the guard or gate `tocarVideo()` on `semMovimento`; add `controls` or one pause toggle; add `focusin`/`focusout` on the carousel; drop `aria-live` during auto-advance and restore it for manual navigation only.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] The five sections have no headings
**What:** `01 / INICIO` through `05 / CONTATO` are `<p class="sec-label">`. The heading outline has no skipped levels and exactly one h1, but it reads: "Desenvolvedor Python" then six project titles then "Vamos conversar". Sobre has no heading. Section 03 has no heading and no accessible name at all. No `aria-labelledby` on any `<section>`, so landmark navigation returns nothing either.
**Why it matters:** a screen-reader user's first move is the heading list, and here it returns a bare project inventory. It also costs SEO and ATS parsing: your most distinctive content is structurally invisible.
**Fix:** promote each `.sec-label` to `<h2>` with styling unchanged, demote the three feature titles to `<h3>` and the grid cards to `<h4>`, add `aria-labelledby` to each section.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable audit`

### [P1] The conversion moment is the least designed block on the page
**What:** three visually identical contact cells with no primary, no CV PDF anywhere on the site, no WhatsApp, no `tel:`, and no reassurance sentence. Preceded by ~280px of empty page, followed by a footer crediting "HTML, Tailwind, Alpine.js e GSAP" at the end of a Python developer's pitch.
**Why it matters:** peak-end rule, and this is the end. A Brazilian recruiter's two fastest actions are downloading a PDF to forward internally and messaging on WhatsApp. Neither exists. Every other section got more compositional care than the one where the hire starts.
**Fix:** make e-mail primary; add "Baixar currículo (PDF)" as a peer action; add WhatsApp; add one line in your own voice about availability and response time.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P2] The JS budget misses by 69% on raw bytes
**What:** measured transfer: Alpine 44,758 + GSAP 72,214 + ScrollTrigger 43,380 + site.js 12,719 = **169.0 KB raw**, against your stated "menos de 100 KB". Gzipped it is 64.7 KB, so the budget holds on the wire and fails on parse. Alpine's entire job is `x-data="{ aberto: false }"` on one mobile menu. GSAP plus ScrollTrigger's entire job is a progress-bar width, a 0.5s wipe and a count-up, and site.js already uses IntersectionObserver twice. Under reduced motion GSAP is still downloaded and parsed to create zero ScrollTriggers.
**Why it matters:** this is the one engineering claim on the page a recruiter can verify in ten seconds. On a page arguing "não gosto de sistema lento", 116 KB of animation library for a scroll bar is the counter-argument.
**Fix:** drop both CDN bundles and Alpine, reimplement all three in site.js with IntersectionObserver plus one scroll handler plus one rAF loop. Roughly 40 lines, under 20 KB, and it removes three third-party origins from the critical path.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable optimize`
